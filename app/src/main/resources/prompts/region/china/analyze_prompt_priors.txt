Indian / Chinese & Asian food priors (optimised for Chinese home cooking, cafeteria meals, and takeout):
- Prefer familiar Chinese dish names that match the plate; use clear regional or cooking-style markers
  when present (steamed rice / 米饭, fried rice / 蛋炒饭, Lanzhou beef noodles / 兰州牛肉面,
  wontons / 馄饨, dumplings / 水饺 / 煎饺, baozi / 包子, mantou / 馒头, jianbing / 煎饼果子,
  youtiao / 油条, congee / 粥, hotpot / 火锅, malatang / 麻辣烫, etc.).
- Staples & carbs: steamed white/brown rice (米饭/糙米饭), wheat or rice noodles (面条/米粉/米线),
  steamed buns (馒头/花卷), stuffed buns (肉包/菜包/小笼包), dumplings (水饺/锅贴),
  scallion pancakes (葱油饼/手抓饼), sweet potato / corn (红薯/玉米).
- Home-style & stir-fry dishes: tomato scrambled eggs (番茄炒蛋), Kung Pao chicken (宫保鸡丁),
  shredded pork with garlic sauce (鱼香肉丝), braised pork belly (红烧肉), twice-cooked pork (回锅肉),
  stir-fried beef with peppers (小炒黄牛肉/青椒牛柳), Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐), steamed fish (清蒸鱼),
  braised chicken (黄焖鸡), poached/roasted chicken breast (白切鸡/鸡胸肉).
- Vegetables & sides: stir-fried greens / bok choy / broccoli (清炒时蔬/上海青/西兰花),
  shredded potato (酸辣土豆丝), cucumber salad (拍黄瓜), seaweed & egg drop soup (紫菜蛋花汤).
- Breakfast & drinks: soy milk (豆浆), boiled/tea eggs (水煮蛋/茶叶蛋), youtiao (油条),
  steamed buns, oats/milk, bubble tea / coffee.
- Cooking fats: when the dish is stir-fried (炒), braised (红烧/焖), pan-fried (煎), or deep-fried (炸)
  and cooking oil is not stated, include "cooking oil" (食用油) as its OWN ingredient with a typical
  home/restaurant amount (often ~1–2 tsp / 5–10 g).
- Naming: Always use Simplified Chinese dish names in "dish_name" (e.g. "番茄炒蛋", "宫保鸡丁", "白米饭", "红烧牛肉面"). Break
  bento boxes, cafeteria trays, and multi-dish meals into named components (米饭, 主荤菜,
  炒时蔬, 汤, 食用油) rather than "mixed plate".

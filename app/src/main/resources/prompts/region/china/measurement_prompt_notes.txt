Portion / measurement language (China):
- Prefer household units users type: bowl (碗) for rice/noodles/soup/porridge, liang (两 = 50 g raw / ~125 g cooked staple),
  piece/count (个/只/根) for baozi, mantou, dumplings (水饺), eggs, youtiao, and plate/portion (盘/份) for stir-fries. Still output weight_g in grams.
- Countable staples without a count → CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED (how many dumplings/baozi/mantou/bowls of rice…).
- "一碗" / "1 bowl" / "两个" / "二两" map to standard household unit priors.
- Never treat a leading count as grams ("4 almonds" / "4颗杏仁" ≠ 4 g).
